How We Experience the Moment

For all the change around us, one thing remains constant: People still want to come together to have incredible experiences, but in ways that feel right for the moment. A drink at the end of the day with friends. A night out at a musical. Cheering on your team at home or in the stands. These are the rituals that shape how we relax, unwind, connect, and celebrate.

Great spirits have always been part of that equation, but now more than ever they aren’t just something to drink, but are more thoughtfully designed to fit any drinking occasion for how consumers choose to enjoy spirits today. And with Suntory Global Spirits, they are shaped by a relentless commitment to quality and craftsmanship.

It’s also clear how these occasions have shifted. They start earlier. They move more fluidly. They happen at home, with friends, at concerts and sporting events, each one defined less by routine than by intention. Increasingly, people choose drinks to fit the moment rather than the category.

Today’s drinkers are indeed becoming more intentional, choosing spirits that feel right for the occasion and looking for convenience, flavor, and refreshment without losing sight of what matters most: spending time together and having fun.

This is a celebration of how we experience those moments today—and how Suntory Global Spirits brings them to life, with a portfolio designed to meet them wherever they happen, from everyday rituals to exceptional occasions.

“Suntory Time” is a simple idea rooted in the belief that there is a right moment for everything. Today, that idea feels more relevant than ever. The moment comes first. What you drink follows. That’s Suntory Time.

Why the Night Starts Earlier

Evenings now start earlier. What was once a late-night culture has shifted, where drinks begin with a focus on convenience and refreshment, and at the right price point. People are paying closer attention to balancing enjoyment with everything that comes next. This shift is reshaping how people consume alcohol. More flexibility, more occasions, more ways to enjoy a drink without committing to the entire night—where more refreshing options and high-quality ready-to-serves thoughtfully build to keep the moment going.

The Reason We Go Out

The Moment is the Destination

We rarely go out simply for a drink. We go out for what the drink makes possible: connection, discovery, celebration, joy, and shared experience. A game with friends. A concert. A dinner that turns into something exceptional. A reason to step out, to be around people, to feel part of something bigger. The bar or restaurant is part of it, but so is everything around it: new places, new flavors, familiar faces in a different context. At its best, going out offers something simple and essential—connection, discovery, and a sense of occasion. From neighborhood spots to larger cultural moments, the experience leads.

Drink Less, Drink Better

Why Quality is King

Moderation has evolved into something more considered. It’s not about abstaining, but choosing with purpose. Fewer drinks, better made, better suited to the moment. One expertly crafted cocktail or special whisky over ice instead of two. A pour worth slowing down for. A drink that fits the setting, whether at home or at the bar. Expectations are higher. With fewer drinks, each one matters more. It’s a shift toward quality, where heritage, craftsmanship, and balance make every pour count.
